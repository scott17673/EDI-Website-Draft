Our Services

Millwrighting

Industrial Millwrighting for Manufacturing Facilities

Full-scope millwrighting for plants across the GTA and Southern Ontario. We handle machine maintenance, equipment installs, and modifications, plus conveyor work, rotating equipment, shutdown turnarounds, and outage support. Our 433A Red Seal certified millwrights work out of our Milton shop with field crews dispatched across the region. Whether it is a single machinery swap, a precision laser alignment, or a full plant turnaround, the crew that shows up knows the scope before they walk through the door.

  • Equipment installs, machine maintenance, rebuilds, and upgrades
  • Heavy rigging, demolition, and complete plant relocations
  • Turn-key installations and machinery moves
  • Conveyor work, rotating equipment, bearing and shaft replacement
  • Machine maintenance programs, shutdown turnarounds, and outage support
  • Laser alignment, precision leveling, and equipment calibration
  • Shift coverage and skilled trade-labour supplement
  • On-site and in-shop capability

Fabrication & Welding

Structural steel, stainless steel, aluminum, and custom fabrication from our 20,000 sq ft shop in Milton. We design it, build it, load it on a flatbed, and install it in the field. Pipe Fitting

Process & Industrial Piping for Manufacturing Facilities

From tie-ins, modifications, and reroutes to full new-scope installs, our TSSA certified team handles in-shop prefabrication and field installation to ASME B31.1 and B31.3 standards. If it carries product, pressure, or process fluid through your facility, we can design it, build it, and put it in.

  • Process, stainless, sanitary, pressure, pneumatic, steam, compressed air, and utility lines
  • Food & beverage, chemical, aggregate, recycling, metals, building products, manufacturing
  • Tie-ins, modifications, reroutes, and full new-scope installs
  • In-shop prefabrication and field installation to ASME B31.1 and B31.3
  • Food-grade sanitary piping and live hot-tap connections
  • Shutdown, turnaround, and outage piping support
